# Brain

The CEO's rules that hold across every project, learned by the CoS from their answers.
Part of the CoS's system prompt (roles.json). The CoS settles anything a line here
already answers, and appends a line only after the CEO approves it. One rule per line;
newest last.

## Always ask me
- Merging to a main branch, deploying, deleting anything remote, spending money.
- Anything a lead would do differently in each of two credible ways and that
  changes what a user sees.

## Learned
- Leads may merge to dev or other integration branches once a human reviewer approved and required checks pass. Merging to a production branch (main/release), deploying, and anything above still ask. A project's own stricter rule wins (approved 2026-11-11, 1111-1117-b917).
